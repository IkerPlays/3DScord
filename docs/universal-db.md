## Short description (one line)

A Discord client for the Nintendo 3DS: chat, voice calls with camera, and screen sharing.

## Long description

3DScord is a Discord client that runs natively on the Nintendo 3DS family. Read and write messages, join voice channels
and calls, and share your camera or your screen, all on the two screens of your console. It is free and open source (GPLv3),
and it is a fork of TriCord by 2b-zipper.

**Chat**

- Servers with folders, channels, forum threads, direct messages and group chats
- Replies, reactions, edits, deletes, pins, spoilers, polls, embeds, stickers and emoji
- @mentions: type `@name` and it is sent as a real mention
- Attachments with a file browser and a preview before sending, GIFs, and animated images (GIF, APNG, Lottie)
- Voice messages and sound files from the chat: Opus, MP3, WAV, FLAC and Ogg Vorbis
- MP4 videos (H.264) play from the message menu
- Search servers, channels and members, and open user profiles

**Voice and video**

- Voice channels and calls with end-to-end encryption (DAVE), per-user volume, push to talk and echo cancellation
- Your camera in a call: inner or outer camera (New 3DS / New 2DS XL, Settings > Voice > Video)
- See the camera and the shared screen of other people in the call
- Go Live: share the top screen, the bottom screen or both as a stream. It keeps working when you go to the HOME Menu
- Notifications outside the app (Home menu entry and LED) on consoles with Luma3DS, when the system keeps the app running

**Your account and your console**

- Log in with a QR code, email and password, or a `token.txt` file; several accounts with quick switching
- Edit your profile, About Me, pronouns, colour, avatar, banner, status and custom status from the console
- Dark, Light, AMOLED, Nintendo, Retro and Midnight themes, a theme editor and custom themes, many languages, left-handed mode and a battery saver
- An in-app updater that shows the release notes and installs new versions

**Requirements**

- A Nintendo 3DS, 2DS, New 3DS or New 2DS XL with custom firmware (Luma3DS) and Wi-Fi
- Chat and voice work on every model; the camera, screen sharing and seeing other people's video need a New 3DS / New 2DS XL
- A Discord account

**Notes**

- This is an unofficial, fan-made client. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Discord Inc. or Nintendo. Third-party clients are not supported by Discord: use it at your own risk and never share your token.
- The original Nintendo DS family (DS, DS Lite, DSi) cannot run it.

the app's own screenshot option) of: the server list, a chat, the call view with a camera, the Go Live screen, and the settings.
