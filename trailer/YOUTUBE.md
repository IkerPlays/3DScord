### Description (copy and paste everything between the lines)

---
Discord on a Nintendo 3DS. Yes, really. 3DScord is a free, open-source Discord client for the Nintendo 3DS family: chat, voice calls with end-to-end encryption, front and back camera, and screen sharing (Go Live) that keeps working even when you leave the app.

Download (free): https://github.com/IkerPlays/3DScord/releases/latest
Source code and guides: https://github.com/IkerPlays/3DScord

WHAT IT DOES
• Chat with replies, reactions, @mentions and voice messages
• Voice calls with end-to-end encryption (DAVE)
• Camera in calls: inner and outer camera
• Go Live: share the top screen, the bottom screen or both, even from the HOME Menu
• Plays MP3, FLAC, WAV and Ogg files from the chat
• Notifications, themes and more

WHAT YOU NEED
• A Nintendo 3DS family console with custom firmware (Luma3DS) to install the .cia or run the .3dsx
• Video features (camera, screen sharing) need a New Nintendo 3DS / New 2DS XL
• A Discord account and Wi-Fi

NOTES
• This is a fan-made homebrew app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by Discord Inc. or Nintendo. Third-party clients are not supported by Discord: use it at your own risk.

## Why it should do better than a plain screen recording

- **First 3 seconds:** one clear, funny idea ("Your friends are on Discord. You're holding a Nintendo 3DS."), no logo wait.
- **Beat-matched:** the drop of the music is the logo; every scene change lands on a bar.
- **One idea per scene,** 8 seconds each, big text, readable on a phone.
- **Real features only** and an honest "what you need" in the description (it keeps the comments friendly and the watch time high).
- **Rewatch value:** the call scene switches from the inner to the outer camera, the live scene jumps to the HOME Menu.
