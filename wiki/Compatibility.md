# Compatibility

| Console | Chat and voice | Camera, screen sharing, seeing other people's video |
|---|---|---|
| New Nintendo 3DS / New 3DS XL | Yes | Yes |
| New Nintendo 2DS XL | Yes | Yes (tested here) |
| Nintendo 3DS / 3DS XL | Yes | No: too slow for the video |
| Nintendo 2DS | Yes | No: too slow for the video |
| Nintendo DS, DS Lite, DSi | No | No |

## Why the video needs a New 3DS / New 2DS XL

The video is encoded and decoded by the processor (the 3DS has no usable hardware codec for this). The New models have a faster processor and an extra core,
which the app uses. On the other consoles the **Video (experimental)** setting is hidden and the video features stay off.

## The original Nintendo DS family

The DS, DS Lite and DSi use a different processor and system. 3DS apps (CIA and 3DSX) cannot run on them, and there is no plan to port 3DScord.

## Custom firmware

3DScord is homebrew: it needs a console with custom firmware (Luma3DS is the one it is tested on). Some features need Luma3DS services:

- Notifications outside the app (Home menu entry and LED)
- Sharing the screen from the HOME Menu or other apps
