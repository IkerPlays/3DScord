# FAQ

**Does it work on the Nintendo DS?** No. The DS, DS Lite and DSi cannot run 3DS apps. See [[Compatibility]].

**Does it work on the old 3DS and 2DS?** Chat and voice, yes. The camera, screen sharing and seeing video need a New 3DS / New 2DS XL.

**Do I need custom firmware?** Yes (Luma3DS is the tested one). It is homebrew.

**Can I get banned?** Discord does not support third-party clients and its terms forbid some uses of them. 3DScord is made for educational purposes and you use it at your own risk.

**Is my token safe?** It stays on your SD card, in `/3ds/3DScord`. Never share that folder or your token.

**Why is the video so small?** The console encodes and decodes it in software, so it uses small pictures and a low frame rate that adjust to the load.

**Can I share the screen of a game?** The Go Live stream can capture the screen while you are in the HOME Menu or another app, if the system lets 3DScord keep running (experimental). See [[Voice and video]].

**Why no notifications when the app is closed?** Nothing runs when the app is closed. See [[Notifications]].

**How do I update?** The app offers updates at startup. See [[Installation]].

**How do I report a bug?** Save a report in **Settings > Diagnostics** and open an [issue](https://github.com/IkerPlays/3DScord/issues).

**Where do the logo and sounds come from?** They are original and generated with the scripts in `tools/brand/`.

## Does a screen share carry the sound of the game?

No. The 3DS gives every app its own audio output and offers no way to record what another app (or the HOME Menu) plays, so a Go Live stream has picture only. The people in the call hear you through your microphone as usual.
